Family protection, kept simple. See your family on a map, and send an SOS with one tap when you need help.

What BrentGuard does

  • Family map — see family members on a full-screen map, with their real address on top and coordinates underneath.
  • Multiple circles — each family circle has its own code. Your location goes to every circle you joined. SOS and check-ins go to your active circle only.
  • SOS button — tap SOS on the main screen to send an SOS to your active circle. SOS messages can be dismissed on your own phone only, so no one can hide someone else's SOS.
  • Address + coordinates — a real street address when one is available, with exact coordinates underneath, so you never lose the precise spot.
  • Last Known Location — if a member loses signal, their last known spot stays on the map, faded, with the time and “signal lost” — never shown as live.
  • Save name — in Details, type your name and tap Save name; your bottom icon updates right away.
  • Refresh — a refresh button gets a fresh location, posts it to your circles, and pulls your active circle.

How to install

  1. Tap Download BrentGuard above.
  2. When it asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open BrentGuard, create or join a circle with your family code, and turn on location sharing.
  4. Install over the top for updates — do not uninstall. Uninstalling wipes your circles, settings, and permissions, and you would have to set everything up again.

Installing an unverified app

BrentGuard is not on the Play Store yet, so Android may call it an unknown or unverified app. That is expected — it just means you downloaded it from here instead of from the store, not that anything is wrong with it.

  • Tap Download BrentGuard, then tap Install. If Android blocks it, it will usually offer a button to go to Settings — tap it.
  • Or go yourself: Settings > Security and privacy > Install unknown apps (on some phones: More security settings > Install unknown apps). Pick the browser you downloaded from, like Chrome or Samsung Internet, and turn on Allow from this source.
  • Then go back and tap Install again.
  • If Google Play Protect pops up a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The exact words can be a little different on different phones.
  • After it is installed, you can turn that browser permission back off if you want — BrentGuard will still work.
  • Install over the top for updates — do not uninstall. Uninstalling wipes your circles, settings, and permissions, and you would have to set everything up again.

Privacy, in plain words

No ads. No analytics. No data sold. No pricing in the app.

BrentGuard needs your location to show your family where you are, and it uses the family circle relay to share that location only with people in your circles. The map tiles and the address lookup (Esri, with OpenStreetMap as a backup) see the location being looked up, but no names are sent with it. The relay stores encrypted location data — it cannot read your family key or your readable locations.
